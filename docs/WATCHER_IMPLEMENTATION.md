## Original Watcher design constraint

The Watcher remains an original Three Realms creature concept: extremely tall and thin, narrow charcoal body, very long arms/fingers, small nearly featureless head, pale eyes, hunched posture, and restrained stalker behavior. Verity, SCP, and other recognizable designs must not replace this concept.

## Production decision

Obtain and inspect the Skinny Monster 2 archive, verify its included license/attribution and formats, then decide whether it can be technically converted without artistic redesign. Until then no Watcher texture/model is promoted and no final creature package is built.
